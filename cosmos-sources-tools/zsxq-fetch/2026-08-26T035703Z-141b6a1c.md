## Why the change was needed

The authenticated timeline collector treated an anchor without an explicit URL target as a webpage attachment. It emitted an empty `original_url`, while the runner correctly requires every webpage attachment to carry a non-empty HTTP(S) URL, so the collector-to-runner handoff stopped before extraction.

## Reproduction

1. Open a signed-in Knowledge Planet timeline containing topic text rendered as an anchor with no `href` attribute.
2. Collect a complete historical Beijing day with the v7 browser adapter.
3. Submit the returned inventory to `record-inventory`.
4. Observe that the collector emits `type: web` with an empty `original_url`, and the runner rejects that field as a non-empty-string contract violation.
