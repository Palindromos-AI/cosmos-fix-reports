- Plugin: `cosmos-sources-tools` 0.8.0

- Status: repaired

## Why

Independent review found that a timeline file download could rely on a DOM topic index, file ordinal, and filename after collection. If the live timeline reordered before download, another same-named card could be opened.

## Reproduction

1. Collect two timeline topics whose author, displayed timestamp, body, and attachment filename are identical but whose file-card identities differ.
2. Reorder the topics before invoking the download helper with the original DOM index.
3. The previous helper could identify the wrong same-named occurrence by position; the corrected helper rejects the stale target.

## Diff summary

The download contract now requires every PDF or HTML card to expose a safe nonempty stable platform ID. Timeline targets also carry their collected topic context. Download helpers re-resolve the card by stable ID inside the verified topic and confirm both ID and filename immediately before opening it. Missing or changed identities fail closed.
