## Why

The ZSXQ file-card collector supported PDF attachments only. HTML attachments could neither complete the same official download handshake nor enter the local extraction, cache, evidence, audit, and reader pipeline.

## Reproduction

1. Inspect a ZSXQ timeline topic or detail page containing an `.html` or `.htm` file card.
2. The previous v6 browser contract rejects the card as a file-attachment mismatch or omits it from inventory.
3. Even if a downloaded HTML record is supplied manually, the previous runner rejects `type: html`.
