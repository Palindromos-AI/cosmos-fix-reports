## Why the change was needed

The timeline collector applied strict individual file-card validation to every visible topic, so an unsupported or malformed card outside the requested date could block an otherwise complete target-day archive. Off-target topics should serve only as chronology evidence, while target-date and unscoped collection remain strict.

## Reproduction

1. Inspect a timeline with an explicit target date and enough topics to cross below that date.
2. Place an unsupported or malformed file card on a topic outside the target date.
3. Before the fix, the collector returns `FILE_ATTACHMENT_MISMATCH`; after the fix, it ignores only that off-target individual card while preserving strict failure for the same card on the target date or without `targetDate`.
